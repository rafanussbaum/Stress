\section{Rune Exercise 1: U--Position}

This bending position exists in many systems, as for instance in yoga or the rune exercises. 
They stimulate the head--nerve center.

\subsection*{Starting Position}

Stand with your feet parallel and shoulder width apart. 
Then slowly lift your arms above your head.

\subsection*{Execution}

Inhale as deeply as possible and then slowly exhale. 
Bend simultaneously the torso and head forward and down, till the torso is in a horizontal position.
The arms and finger should point downwards, towards the earth (see picture).
Slightly bend your knees, in order for your back to be more relaxed.
Shift your weight more and more towards your hands.
Stay in this position for the duration of 10 breaths. Increase this length daily by one breath, till you reached an optimal duration of 30 breaths.

If you are not able to execute the exercise due to health reasons, then execute the exercise while being seated. 
In this case, sit with a straight spine and lift your arms above your head, while calmly and deeply inhaling.
While exhaling, bend your torso as much forward as possible. 
Let your arms hang towards the floor, left and right of your legs.
Breathe and increase the length as described above.
