\section{Rune Exercise 1: U--Position}

\label{Ex:RuneU}
\begin{figure}[htb!]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[height=10cm]{Rune_U.jpg}\label{ex:runeU}
	\caption{The Rune Exercises: Rune U}
\end{figure}


This bending position exists in many systems, as for instance in yoga or the rune exercises. 
They stimulate the head--nerve center.

\subsection*{Starting Position}

Stand with your feet parallel and shoulder width apart. 
Then slowly lift your arms above your head.

\subsection*{Execution}

Inhale as deeply as possible and then slowly exhale. 
Bend simultaneously the torso and head forward and down, till the torso is in a horizontal position.
The arms and finger should point downwards, towards the earth (see picture~\ref{ex:runeU}).
Slightly bend your knees, in order for your back to be more relaxed.
Shift your weight more and more towards your hands.
Stay in this position for the duration of 10 breaths. Increase this length daily by one breath, till you reached an optimal duration of 30 breaths.

If you are not able to execute the exercise due to health reasons, then execute the exercise while being seated. 
In this case, sit with a straight spine and lift your arms above your head, while calmly and deeply inhaling.
While exhaling, bend your torso as much forward as possible. 
Let your arms hang towards the floor, left and right of your legs.
Breathe and increase the length as described above.

\section{Rune Exercise 2: I--Position}

\label{Ex:RuneI}
\begin{figure}[htb!]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[height=10cm]{Rune_I.jpg}\label{ex:runeI}
	\caption{The Rune Exercises: Rune I}
\end{figure}

The exercise of the rune I served the Germanic peoples to increase the mental and physical power of resistance.
Especially his exercise is also known as the power exercise. In measurements it is shown as highly effective at relaxing the main interference fields and muscle tension. 

\subsection*{Starting Position}

Stand up or sit, if possible facing the west.

\subsection*{Execution}

Slowly lift your hands above your head.
The palm of your hands are facing each other, in a distance of about 2/3 of a foot (20 cm, see picture~\ref{Ex:RuneI}).
Breathe slowly and deeply in a regular fashion. 
Stay in this position for 10 breaths. Slowly increase this duration by one breath more per day, till you reach the ideal practice time of 30 breaths.

\section{Rune exercise 3: Y--Position}

\label{Ex:RuneY}
\begin{figure}[htb!]
	\centering
	\includegraphics[height=10cm]{Rune_Y.jpg}\label{ex:runeY}
	\caption{The Rune Exercises: Rune Y}
\end{figure}
 
 The Y--exercise comes from the rune exercises. It serves in ancient times to transmutate sexual energy and to unlock higher spheres and the subtle worlds.
 
 \subsection*{Starting Position}
 
Continue from the position of exercise 2, without stopping.
 
 \subsection*{Execution}
 
 Let both arms slowly sink down to the point where your body looks like the letter "Y". 
 Your palms are flat and turned upwards (see picture`\ref{ex:runeY}).
 In this exercise pay attention to breathe in a relaxed and calm way. 
 Stay in this position at first for 10 breaths and slowly increase till length by one breath a day to eventually achieve 30 breaths.
