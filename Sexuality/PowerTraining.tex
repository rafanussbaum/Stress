\section{The Power Training}

\subsection*{Build Up Exercise}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item While standing up, tense your PC muscle for three seconds. 
	This is being done by constricting the sphincter around the anus.
	If you are doing it right, there's a feeling as if the whole pelvic floor would pull up.
	Inhale slowly and calmly.
	IF three seconds seem too long, as the PC muscle is still too weak, start with one or two seconds.
	On exhale, totally relax the muscle.
	
	In the beginning, do this about ten times or as long as you can, but not longer than ten minutes.
	
	\item When the force and stamina increases (after about a week), 
	increase the tensing period so that you can count to ten in your head.
	Then, relax as long as you tensed the muscle. 
	Don't forget this relaxation period and every single time consciously relax your PC muscle.
	This is as important as the flexing of the muscle.
	
	Do this a few times, to a maximum of five minutes and then
	relax for a minute.
	Then, flex and relax the muscle ten times as fast as you can.
	Repeat the first part (counting to ten, while slowly flexing/relaxing) 
	and then do another repetition of ten quick flex/relax.
	
	If you have time and like it, you can repeat the whole process over again.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Summary:}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Flex and relax the PC muscle for three seconds. 
	Inhale while flexing, exhale while relaxing the muscle. 
	Ten or more times, not more than ten minutes.
	\item (After about a week of training:) 
	Flex and inhale while counting to ten, then relax while you  exhale and count to ten.
	Short period of relaxing, then ten times quickly flex and relax.
	Repeat two to three times.
\end{enumerate}
