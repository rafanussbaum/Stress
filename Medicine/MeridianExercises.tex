The state of the meridians has a direct influence on our physical and mental health. We might have made the experience in life how our life's quality depends on having enough energy at every moment.
But we should now just look at the energy present in our system but also how well it can pass the meridians.

We can correct imbalances and energy blockages by changing the way diet and execute the meridian exercises on a regular base.

\section{The Distribution of the Meridians on the Body's Surface}

The 12 main meridians are symmetrically distributed on the left and right half of the body.

The \emph{YIN meridians} are along the extremities on the inner side of the body (for instance: corresponds to the side which bends on the arms),
the \emph{YANG meridians} are located on the outer side of the body.
