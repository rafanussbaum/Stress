\section{The Liver Meridian}\label{meridian!liver}

The liver is a truly amazing organ. It delivers many functions, as storing of energy, cleaning of the blood, and synthesis of immune cells and digestive enzymes.
In the far eastern view, the liver is \emph{the seat of the soul}.
The emotion related to liver and gall bladder is \emph{anger}.
Too intense anger hurts the liver, equanimity leads to relaxation and better liver function.

\subsection{Energy Deficiency of the Liver}\label{meridian!liver!energy deficiency}

	\begin{center}

\end{center}

Insufficient liver energy easily causes \emph{fatigue}\index{symptom!fatigue}, as the liver doesn't produce any glycogen anymore, a fuel produces and stored in the liver.
People with low liver energy suffer from \emph{vertigo}\index{symptom!vertigo} and \emph{tired eyes}\index{symptom!eyes!tired}, there's also a heightened risk of \emph{accidents}\index{symptom!accident prone}.
There is a slight poisoning of the body, as the liver can't purify the blood anymore.
These people tens to catch a hepatitis and other sever liver diseases\index{symptom!liver diseases}\index{symptom!hepatitis} and have a hard time recovering from them.
People with low liver energy suffer from feverish afflictions, the sexual activity is weak\index{symptom!sexual activiy!weak}, it leads to erectile dysfunction\index{symptom!erectile dysfunction} and prostate problems\index{symptom!prostate!problems}.
