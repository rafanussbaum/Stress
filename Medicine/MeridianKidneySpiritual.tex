\section{The Meridian of the Small Intestines}\label{meridian!small intestine}

The small intestine have the task to take in nutrients and make them accessible to the blood stream.
That is also an important task on the abstract level.
The small intestine pulls these important substances out of that rough, untreated matter and it refines them for us.
Also in your life, there's nothing more important than to recognize, whats valuable and how we can use it.
The quality of our blood 9the supply of the blood with the corresponding nutrients) depends on the nutrition and the functions of the small intestines.
If the food is lacking important nutrients or there's too much fat and cholesterol, which coat the villi of the small intestine, we can't get enough nutrients.
As a result, the cells suffer shortage.
They have to deplete the neighboring tissues from nutrients, the bones and teeth.
the small intestine also absorbs iron, which supports the oxygen transport  through the body to every single cell.
When the iron levels are low, the blood's capacity to transport oxygen suffers in turn.
A healthy intake of iron depends mainly on a healthy functioning of the  small intestine.

\subsection{Energy Deficiency of the  Small Intestines}\label{meridian!small intestine!energy deficiency}

	\begin{center}

If there's not enough energy in the small intestine, there will be difficulties with the intake of nutrients.
such a patient will suffer from a certain extend of \emph{malnutrition}.
Even more likely, he's \emph{anemic} and \emph{chronically tired}.
The weakness is most pronounced in the hips, the legs and lower back. also the middle lacks the necessary energy.
Very likely it will lead to a congestion of the blood in the small intestines.
If this state isn't remedied, health will suffer sooner or later.
A weak small intestine leads to other digestion problems, from congestion to appendicitis.
For women, disturbances of the small intestine lead to chronic menstruation problems.
Also the premenstrual syndrome as pain and cysts in the ovaries can be a result of insufficient energy in the small intestines.
Any type of problems with the intestines often lead to headaches and can cause migraine.
