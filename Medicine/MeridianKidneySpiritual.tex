\section{The Kidney Meridian}\label{meridian!kidneys}

As mentioned, the kidneys purify the blood. 
The adrenals are located on the upper end of the kidneys and produce adrenaline and have the same job.
Adrenaline is the hormone, which is responsible for the immediate reaction in crisis situations.
In the far eastern medicine it says, that the kidneys have the \emph{control over fear and courage}.
They also contain spiritual gifts.
Therefore, the kidneys are counted among the most important organs.


\subsection{Energy Deficiency of the Kidneys}\label{meridian!kidneys!energy deficiency}

	\begin{center}
	\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{MeridianKidneyD.png}\label{img:MerKidneyD}	
\end{center}


Patients with a weak kidney energy often have a brownish hue to their skin\index{symptom!skin!brownish} and it lacks the suppleness and elasticity\index{symptom!skin!elasticity lacking}.
These people often have bad circulation, especially in the hips and their middle.
They suffer from a \emph{constant urge to urinate}\index{symptom!urge to urinate} 
and form pain in the lower back.
The kidneys also regulate the sexual organs and therefore influence our hormonal balance.
With a low kidney energy, the \emph{sex drive} is also \emph{weak}\index{symptom!sex drive weak}.
People with a low kidney energy have difficulties to find a \emph{deep sleep}\index{symptom!deep sleep}.
The kidneys also influence the state of our bones.
Weak kidneys often cause diseases like \emph{osteoporosis}\index{symptom!osteoporosis}.
Bones break easier, affected people are often more \emph{accident prone}.
The kidneys are also responsible for the organs, which are related to the sense of \emph{hearing}.
In case of a kidney weakness, the circulation in the ears is insufficient, which can lead to \emph{hearing losses in the higher frequency range}\index{symptom!hearing!loss} or sounds in your ears (tinnitus)\index{symptom!tinnitus}.

\subsection{Energy Excess of the Kidneys}\label{meridian!kidneys!excess energy}

\begin{center}

People suffering form excessive kidney energy, often suffer from a \emph{constant thirst}\index{symptom!thirst, constanly},  
\emph{ringing in the ears}\index{symptom!ears, ringing}, a \emph{weakness of hearing}\index{symptom!hearing!weakness} and a \emph{stiffness in the lower back and torso}\index{symptom!back, lower!siff}\index{symptom!torso!stiff}.
The \emph{urine is dark}\index{symptom!urine!dark}, they have a bad taste in their mouths and \emph{bad breath}\index{symptom!breath!bad}.
As a result of this excess energy, a \emph{chronic exhaustion}\index{symptom!exhaustion, chronic} results.
The skin is often colored darker\index{symptom!skin!darker}, especially below the eyes.
