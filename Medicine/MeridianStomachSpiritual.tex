\section{The Stomach Meridian}\label{meridian!stomach}

The task of the stomach is to absorb the partially digested (well chewed) food and to prepare it for the small intestines.
The stomach excretes an acid, which breaks up the food and makes sure it can be  more readily absorbed by the blood in the small intestines.
We can't just ignore the stomach. Every problem with the stomach is an impairment and chronic stomach problems will handicap us all day long. 

\subsection{Energy Deficiency of the  Stomach}\label{meridian!stomach!energy deficiency}

	\begin{center}
	\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{MeridianStomachD}\label{img:MerStomachD}	
\end{center}

When the energy of the stomach is missing, you have little appetite\index{symptom!appetite!low}, find fault with the food\index{symptom!food!finding fault} and suffer from chronic \emph{dysfunction of the stomach acid secretion}\index{symptom!stomach!acid secretion problems}.
Often the \emph{legs are heavy}\index{symptom!legs!heavy feeling}, the \emph{body tires easily}\index{symptom!tired!easily}, you constantly have stomach problems\index{symptom!stomach!problem, constantly}.
The slightest mistake in your diet makes you suffer for hours from stomach problems\index{symptom!diet!sensitive stomach}.
Generally speaking, problems of the stomach are always related to digestive problems, like congestion\index{symptom!digestion!congestion}, diarrhea\index{symptom!digestion!diarrhea} or cramps of the intestines\index{symptom!digestion!cramps, intestines}.

\subsection{Energy Excess of the  Stomach}\label{meridian!stomach!excess energy}

\begin{center}
	\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{MeridianStomachS}\label{img:MerStomachS}

Excess energy of the stomach leads paying especial attention to the stomach.
People with too much stomach energy tend to \emph{eat too much}\index{symptom!overeating, tendendy to}, in some cases \emph{a loss of appetite}\index{symptom!appetite!loss of} results.
At times, there's pain in the shoulders\index{symptom!shoulders!pain}.
The circulation of the blood isn't working great, especially in the legs\index{symptom!legs!circulation, bad}.
The skin easily gets dry\index{symptom!skin!dry} and rough\index{symptom!skin!rough}.
There's a tendency to anemia (not enough blood)\index{symptom!anemia}, for women also chronic symptoms in the area of the genitals\index{symptom!genital syptoms, women}.
