\section{The Stomach Meridian}\label{meridian!large intestines}

The task of the stomach is to absorb the partially digested (well chewed) food and to prepare it for the small intestines.
The stomach excretes an acid, which breaks up the food and makes sure it can be  more readily absorbed by the blood in the small intestines.
We can't just ignore the stomach. Every problem with the stomach is an impairment and chronic stomach problems will handicap us all day long. 

\subsection{Energy Deficiency of the  Stomach}\label{meridian!large intestines!energy deficiency}

	\begin{center}

When the energy of the stomach is missing, you have little appetite, find fault with the food and suffer from chronic \emph{dysfunction of the stomach acid secretion}.
Often the \emph{legs are heavy}, the \emph{body tires easily}, you constantly have stomach problems.
The slightest mistake in your diet makes you suffer for hours from stomach problems.
Generally speaking, problems of the stomach are always related to digestive problems, like congestion, diarrhea or cramps of the intestines.

People with too much stomach energy tend to \emph{eat too much}, in some cases \emph{a loss of appetite} results.
