\section{The Large Intestine Meridian}\label{meridian!large intestines}

The main task of the large intestine are the excretion of waste products and the absorption of water and different nutrients (the most important organ of absorption is the small intestine).
There's a mutual relation between the large intestines and the lungs.
Problems of the large intestines often affect the lungs and the sinuses.
Large intestines and the lungs are the organs, which are \emph{related to sorrows and pain}.
	If the large intestines and lungs are not working properly, there's the tendency to hold on to experiences related with sorrows and sadness and similar emotions.

If the large intestine is missing the required energy, people typically suffer from congestion; there's a \emph{drying up and congestion of the nasal cavities} 
and the \emph{accumulation of mucus in the bronchia}.
When people with a weak large intestine eat food with a lot of fibers, raw or grainy consistency, they often get diarrhea.
They also suffer from a feeling of cold in the stomach region.

\subsection{Energy Excess of the Large Intestine}\label{meridian!large intestines!excess energy}

Too much energy is the region of the large intestines causes \emph{head aches}, the \emph{nose starts running} or \emph{is congested}, there can be \emph{nose bleeds}, \emph{tonsillitis}, \emph{pain on the teeth and gums}, the eyes and the face are pale.
There also are pain in the shoulder and a stiffness in the region of the chest.
Congestion and diarrhea are taking turns, hemorrhoids, a cough and all the symptoms of the \emph{cold} develop.
Problems of the large intestines directly affect the lungs, the bronchia and the nasal cavities.
The reason for that is the excessive energy wanders up in the body, as it can't be excreted towards the bottom.
The body tries to establish equilibrium again, by conducting that energy towards the upper organs. That is where these symptoms in the lungs, throat and nose originate.
