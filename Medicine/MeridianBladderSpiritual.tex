\section{The Bladder Meridian}\label{meridian!bladder}

Bladder and kidneys are related organs.
This is the view of the western as well of the eastern medicine.
Fear is related to the bladder and the kidneys.
The bladder influences the hormone balance, the pituitary gland and the autonomous nervous system.
The bladder also has a direct influence on the sexual organs and the urethrae.
