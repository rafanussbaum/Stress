\section{The Bladder Meridian}\label{meridian!bladder}

Bladder and kidneys are related organs.
This is the view of the western as well of the eastern medicine.
Fear is related to the bladder and the kidneys.
The bladder influences the hormone balance, the pituitary gland and the autonomous nervous system.
The bladder also has a direct influence on the sexual organs and the urethrae.


\subsection{Energy Deficiency of the Bladder}\label{meridian!bladder!energy deficiency}

	\begin{center}
	\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{MeridianBladderD.png}\label{img:MerBladderD}	
\end{center}

Patients with a weak bladder often suffer from \emph{frequent urge to urinate}\index{symptom!unrination urge!fequent}, they don't have the bladder under control, suffer from nervous tension.
These people have a bad circulation, it leads to \emph{cramps in the legs}\index{symptom!legs!cramps} and to a \emph{feeling of cold along the spine and in the region of the buttocks}\index{symptom!spine!cold}\index{symptom!buttocks!cold} (corresponding to the pathway of the bladder meridian: down the back, over the hips and buttocks, down the legs to he feet and the little toes).
It can also lead to problems of the sexual organs. 
A  weakness of the bladder often a cause of \emph{night sweat}\index{symptom!night sweat}.


\subsection{Energy Excess of the Bladder}\label{meridian!bladder!excess energy}

\begin{center}

People with excessive energy in the bladder have at times a\emph{stiff neck and throat}\index{symptom!neck!stiff}.

They feel a \emph{big pressure in the eyes}\index{symptom!eyes!pressure feeling} and in the \emph{head}\index{symptom!head!pressure feeling},
there's often \emph{cramps in the calves}\index{symptom!calves!cramps, often}, an \emph{increased urge to urinate}\index{symptom!urination urge} and often leads to an \emph{inflammation of the prostate}\index{symptom!prostate!inflammation}.
The autonomous nervous system can be overtaxed.
