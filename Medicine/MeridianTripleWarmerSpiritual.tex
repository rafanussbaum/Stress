\section{The Triple Warmer Meridian}\label{meridian!triple warmer}

This meridian goes from the forth finger up the arm to the shoulder, then up the neck, then loops around the upper ear and goes to the temple.
This meridian links three power centers with each other.

\subsection{Energy Deficiency of the Triple Warmer}\label{meridian!triple warmer!energy deficiency}

	\begin{center}
	\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{Meridian3WarmerD.png}\label{img:Mer3WarmerD}	
\end{center}

People with insufficient energy in the triple warmer are very \emph{sensitive to temperature changes and humidity}\index{symptom!temperature chage, sensitivity to}\index{symptom!humidity, sensitivity to}.
They \emph{easily catch a cold}\index{symptom!cold!susceptile to}, have \emph{tired eyes}\index{symptom!eyes!tires} and a \emph{sensitive skin}\index{symptom!skin!sensitive}, so they often react \emph{allergically}\index{symptom!skin!allergies}\index{symptom!hay fever} to pollen and other antigens. 
They often suffer from a pressure in the chest area\index{symptom!chest!pressure} and in the hara center\index{symptom!hara center!pressure}, 
under low blood pressure\index{symptom!blood pressure!low} and pain in the back of the head\index{symptom!head, back!pain} and the temples\index{symptom!temples!pain}.


\subsection{Energy Excess of the Triple Warmer}\label{meridian!triple warmer!excess energy}

\begin{center}
	\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{Meridian3WarmerS.png}\label{img:Mer3WarmerS}	
\end{center}	

People with too much energy in this meridian tend to \emph{inflammation of the lymph knots}\index{symptom!lymph knots!inflamamtion} and a \emph{mucous obstruction of the nose}\index{symptom!nose!mucous obstruction}.
They are very \emph{accident--prone}\index{symptom!accident--prone}, have a \emph{bad circulation of the blood}\index{symptom!circulation of the blood!bad} and often suffer from \emph{itching sensation in the skin}\index{symptom!skin!itching}, pressure in the chest\index{symptom!chest!pressure}, heavy legs\index{symptom!legs!heavy} and inflammations of the gums\index{symptom!gums!inflammation}, mouth\index{symptom!mouth!inflammation} and uterus\index{symptom!uterus!inflammation}.
