\section{The Heart Sac/Pericardium Meridian}\label{meridian!heart sac}

This meridian starts at the armpits, along the arms to the point of the middle finger. 
far eastern medicine says, that he supplies \emph{additional energy for the heart, circulation and the pericardium}.
As this energy supports the blood circulation, it also supports the adequate supply of the body's tissues with oxygen and nutrients.
The problems which arise in this context are similar to the problems of the heart energy, as we saw before.


\subsection{Energy Deficiency of the Heart Sac}\label{meridian!heart sac!energy deficiency}

	\begin{center}
	\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{MeridianHeartsacD.png}\label{img:MerBladderD}	
\end{center}

People with a low energy of the heart sac meridian have \emph{difficulties swallowing}\index{symptom!swallowing, difficult}, they are susceptible for \emph{neck problems}\index{symptom!neck problems} and tonsillitis\index{symptom!tonsillitis}, \emph{palpitation of the heart}\index{symptom!heart!palpitation}, \emph{low blood pressure}\index{symptom!blood pressure!low}, trepidation\index{symptom!trepidation} and even pain in the chest and rip region\index{symptom!chest!pain}\index{symptom!rips!pain}.
Patients suffer from \emph{breathlessness}\index{symptom!breathless} and have at times the impression their whole chest cavity is being being under pressure.


\subsection{Energy Excess of the Heart Sac}\label{meridian!heart sac!excess energy}

\begin{center}
	\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{MeridianHeartsacS.png}\label{img:MerBladderS}	
\end{center}	

Excess energy in this meridian causes strong \emph{heart palpitations}\index{symptom!heart!palpitation}, a \emph{high blood pressure}\index{symptom!blood pressure!high}, \emph{vertigo}\index{symptom!vertigo} and a \emph{chronic tiredness}\index{symptom!tiredness!chronic}.
Patients suffer from \emph{circulatory disturbances}\index{symptom!circulation!disturbances}, a stiffness in the solar plexus\index{symptom!solar plexus!stiffness} and in the hara center\index{symptom!hara center!stiffness}, at times also to stomach aches\index{symptom!stomach!aches}.
\emph{Cramps in the hands and palms}\index{symptom!hands!cramps}\index{symptom!palms!cramps}, {tongue coating}\index{symptom!tongue!coating} and digestive problems\index{symptom!digestion!problems} (with colon cramps)\index{symptom!colon!cramps} are often effects of too much pericardium energy.
