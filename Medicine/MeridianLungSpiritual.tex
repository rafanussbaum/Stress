\section{The Lung Meridian}\label{meridian!lungs}

It purifies the blood by enriching it with oxygen and transporting carbon dioxide away.
In the far eastern medicine it is said that inhaling oxygen is means 
\emph{intake of Ki}\index{Ki}, 
the life energy\index{life energy}\index{energy!Ki}.
If you hold your breath for a short while, then it will be very quickly evident, 
how depended we are on the oxygen form our lungs. 
Looking at this process in an abstract or spiritual way, 
we can say that the lungs take life in. 
As soon as the lung doesn't work properly anymore, our capacity to take in the life also suffers.
This has far reaching consequences on body and mind.
